Artist Spotlight: Frank Ocean
Essential Discography

Frank Ocean rose to prominence in the early 2010s as a member of the collective Odd Future before releasing his breakout mixtape, which introduced his blend of storytelling lyricism and unconventional song structure to a wide audience.

His sound is often cited as a blueprint for the alternative R&B movement, with a sparse atmospheric production, unexpected key and tempo changes with a single song, and vocals that shift registers to match the emotional tone of the lyrics.

Beyond the music itself, he is recognized for controlling how an when his work reaches the public, an approach that influenced how many later artists think about release strategy in the streaming era.
